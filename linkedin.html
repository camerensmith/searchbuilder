💡 Tips

  • Use Remote + Past 24 hours + Under 10 applicants to catch fresh, low-competition listings.
  • The Under X applicants filter works best when combined with Easy Apply.
  • Leave Location blank when using the Remote work type to get global results.
  • Use Exclude Companies to filter out pipeline-spam aggregators. Add any other recurring offenders in the custom field.
  • You can bookmark the generated URL in your browser for quick daily searches.
